# Privacy

Current V0.1 behaviour:

- Data remains on the Android device.
- No advertising SDKs.
- No behavioural analytics.
- No telemetry endpoint.
- No location collection.
- No contact access.
- No account creation.
- No cloud partner sync yet.

Before any remote sync is enabled, the app should document the exact server-side data model, retention, deletion path and encryption design. Sensitive couple content should not be sent to third-party analytics services.
